`place_names.json` is derived from GeoNames cities500, downloaded 2026-09-29 from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip . GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/).

Transformation: case-folded city name and ASCII city name mapped to the distinct US administrative region codes or ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country codes; all cities500 rows retained. Keys prefixed `county:` are US county names from GeoNames admin2Codes.txt (https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin2Codes.txt), downloaded 2026-09-29, mapped to state codes. No coordinates, population, identifiers, or personal data are included. A name with multiple readings retains every reading.
